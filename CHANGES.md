### 1.0.2 (2026090501)

Initial release of the Homeschool dashboard for planning day-based coursework in Day sections courses.

* Dashboard of children and their courses, including the day each child is currently on and any incomplete days.
* Day page listing activities across courses for one day, with each child's completion and submission progress.
* Set or clear timeline reminder dates on selected activities. These are completion expected dates only; assignment due dates and quiz close dates are left unchanged.
* Shift reminder dates forward or backward across all days or a chosen day range, with a preview before applying. Undo is available for 30 minutes.
* Clear reminder dates when reusing a course for a new year, without changing completion settings.
* Add and delete activities from the day page, returning there after editing an activity.
* Optional default time of day for new reminders, and an option to show children's surnames.

Requires the Day sections course format (`format_daysections`).
